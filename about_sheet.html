Stats About

Information about my League of Legends stat tracker.

I decided to create a personal League tracker because I enjoy the game, but also because I feel that everything you do in life can be a lesson, even if it is a video game. You are constantly learning while playing League of Legends, whether it is improving your mechanics, decision-making, or other aspects that you may not always notice right away. I wanted to create something related to the game that I could also use personally, so this website will track my own statistics. League of Legends has many champions, ranks, mechanics, and different statistics connected to the game, so I thought it would be fun to create something that I could look at after each game to see how I performed and how I am improving over time.

This website will primarily act as a personal stat tracking page. I want to be able to keep track of information such as my rank, most played champions, win rate, kills, deaths, assists, and most importantly recent matches or a way to store matches and their records permanently. Having these will make it easier for me to see how I am performing and what areas I could watch for.

I was motivated to create this because it is a passion of mine as well as a way for me to see my weak areas, which is especially important for a competitive game like League of Legends. Being able to look back at these things can help someone improve, and the tracker will be tailored to me rather than the overall player base.

These are the things that are going to be tracked

  • Current rank
  • Most played champion
  • Win rate
  • KDA ratio
  • Recent matches
  • Champion mastery
